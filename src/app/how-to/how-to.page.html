How to Play the Trivia 480 Questions Challenge

Welcome to the Trivia 480 Questions Challenge, a game where your knowledge and quick thinking will be tested over the course of 4 months! Here's how it works:

1. Game Duration:

   * The challenge lasts for 4 months (approximately 120 days). Each day, you will receive 4 new questions to answer.

   * A round is made up of these 4 questions per day, for a total of 480 questions over 4 months.

2. Answering Questions:

   * Each day, you’ll be presented with 4 new questions to answer. You’ll have 24 hours to answer them.

3. How the Questions Appear:

   * The questions will be displayed in a list format, with the newest question at the top. As the days progress, the older questions will shift down, creating a flowing “river” of questions.

4. Scoring and Feedback:

   * For each correct answer, a green check will appear in the upper right corner of the question.

   * If you answer incorrectly, a red X will appear, indicating the mistake.

   * After you answer correctly, you'll also earn a Time Bonus. This bonus will show the seconds remaining in the upper left corner of the question.

   * If you have the Quick Thinking skill, you’ll get an extra 10 seconds added to your Time Bonus.

5. Starting Late:

   * If you start the challenge after it has already begun, don’t worry! You’ll receive all the questions from the past days, and they will stay active for as many days as have passed since the start of the round.

   * For example, if you join 3 days later, you’ll get all the past questions up to that point, and the questions you receive will stay active for 3 days.
